01 PhoneDoctors Professional2025–2026Branding, Motion, Web, Editorial

PhoneDoctors is an Apple device repair shop in Medellín. For its 10th anniversary it needed an identity that told who it is today.

I work there as a one-person creative department: I renewed the identity and built the logo system, consolidated the tagline «Los tesos en la manzana», made the launch piece in motion, redesigned the website and designed editorial pieces and a merch line.

Graphic design, motion, web and creative direction

04 · How I work

From idea to final file

Four clear steps, so you know what to expect from the first message.

  1. 01

    Listen

    You tell me about your brand, your audience and what you need to solve.

  2. 02

    Propose

    I show you a visual direction before production, so you decide with something concrete in hand.

  3. 03

    Design

    I build the pieces: identity, editorial, motion or web, with agreed rounds of revisions.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Organized final files, ready to use, with a guide so your team can apply them.
